«Хата з краю» — сторонні ресурси / third-party assets

Textures (PBR sets), HDRI skies and furniture/garden models in public/hata/ph/
are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 — public domain, no
attribution required; credited here anyway. They are downloaded by
scripts/hata-fetch-assets.py (list: hata/assets-wanted.json).

The first-person tools and gloves (public/hata/models/tools.glb) are built by
hata/blender/tools.py in Blender. Trees, grass, leaves, the haystack, dishes,
fixtures and the house itself are generated in code. All sound is synthesized
with WebAudio at runtime. Font: Rubik (SIL Open Font License) via @fontsource.
