«З гірки!» — сторонні ресурси / third-party assets

Sound effects and the finish-line flag are the Kenney (www.kenney.nl) CC0 files
that «Тікай!» already ships in public/tikai/ (sfx/*.mp3, models/plat/flag.glb),
loaded from there — see public/tikai/CREDITS.txt. CC0 1.0: public domain, no
attribution required.

Everything else (riders and their animation, the sleds, ночви, tube and plywood,
snowmen, drifts, ramps, ice, fishermen, sheep, generators, garlands, panel
blocks, the houses of the узвіз, the church, колиби, mountains, candies,
textures, sky, snow, particles, sounds of the runners and the bells) is
generated in code, as in the other games of this repo.
