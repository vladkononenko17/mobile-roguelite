«Тікай!» — сторонні ресурси / third-party assets

3D models (models/) and sound effects (sfx/) are by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) — public domain, no attribution
required. Taken from:
  - Starter Kit Racing      https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing
      vehicle-truck-*.glb, vehicle-motorcycle.glb, decoration-forest.glb,
      impact, skid, engine
  - Starter Kit 3D Platformer https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-3D-Platformer
      flag.glb, coin, jump, land, fall, break
Sounds were converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 (mono, 64 kbps) for iOS Safari.

Everything else (characters and their animation, buildings, roads, blockposts,
textures, the hryvnia coins, sky and clouds, particles, music, sirens) is
generated in code, as in the other games of this repo.
