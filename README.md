# Neon Hollow: Dustline

> This repo also hosts six more games: **«Не клюёт»** (fishing rage-management roguelite) at `/fishing/` — see [its section](#не-клюёт-fishing) — **«Розшук»** (Ukrainian-language survival stealth roguelite) at `/rozshuk/` — see [its section](#розшук-rozshuk) — **«Чотири стіни»** (a psychological roguelite about four years inside one flat) at `/stiny/` — see [its section](#чотири-стіни-stiny) — **«Тікай!»** (a Subway Surfers–style roguelite runner: escape on foot and by car) at `/tikai/` — see [its section](#тікай-tikai) — **«Ставка»** (a geopolitical strategy roguelite: you are the President of Ukraine) at `/stavka/` — see [its section](#ставка-stavka) — and **«З гірки!»** (a sledding runner roguelite: hitch a train of kids behind your sled and race home through a Ukrainian winter) at `/zhirky/` — see [its section](#з-гірки-zhirky).

A mobile-first comic-wasteland action roguelite inspired by the tactile weapon loop of arcade survival shooters. The game runs directly in the browser and is designed for portrait play on iPhone.

## Vertical slice

- Phaser 3 + TypeScript + Vite architecture
- Touch joystick, keyboard movement, hold-to-fire and optional one-thumb AUTO mode
- R-12 rifle with magazine, reload, recoil, spread, and critical hits
- Raider, ranged spitter, brute elite, and a boss encounter
- Ninety-second enemy rush; defeat the Foreman to complete the contract
- Three starting rifle modules (damage, extended magazine, critical chance)
- Scrap XP and three-choice field modifications
- Original procedural inked sprites, four-frame hero walk, muzzle flash, halftone UI, damage numbers, and camera shake
- Physics and weapon timers freeze during pause, upgrade selection, and results
- Installable PWA and automatic GitHub Pages deployment

## Controls

- **Mobile:** left joystick to move; hold **FIRE** for assisted aiming, drag it to aim manually, tap **RELOAD**
- **One-thumb:** toggle **AUTO** to shoot automatically while moving
- **Desktop:** WASD or arrow keys, Space to fire, R to reload
- **Pause:** top-right button, P, or Escape

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

## Build

```bash
npm run build
```

Node 22.18+ is required. `npm test` runs 17 simulation and scene-lifecycle regression tests. The scene tests use physics/UI doubles; they do not replace real-browser checks or device performance testing.

GitHub Actions runs the tests, checks TypeScript, builds Vite, and publishes only `dist/`. Fonts are self-hosted. The service worker precaches versioned build assets and limits cache cleanup to Neon Hollow caches. Offline play requires one complete online load.

## Modules

- `src/game/GameScene.ts`: combat, enemy behavior and scene lifecycle
- `src/game/Weapon.ts`: independently tested weapon simulation
- `src/game/TextureFactory.ts`, `World.ts`: original procedural art
- `src/game/config.ts`, `types.ts`, `math.ts`: tuning and shared rules
- `src/main.ts`, `styles.css`: DOM HUD, touch controls and menus
- `scripts/build-sw.mjs`: cache manifest generated from the production build

Version 0.1's Canvas implementation remains recoverable in Git history. Full rooms, multiple weapons, loot inventories, persistent progression, sound, and a production sprite atlas are not included in this vertical slice.

The current art is an original code-drawn comic wasteland treatment used to validate feel and performance. The next production pass will replace it with a cohesive animated sprite atlas while keeping the same gameplay systems.

## «Не клюёт» (fishing)

A second vertical slice in the same repo and toolchain (TypeScript + Vite, DOM HUD in the same comic-ink style). The world is real-time 3D rendered with Three.js, since Phaser is 2D-only. Served at `/mobile-roguelite/fishing/`.

You fish next to your friend Tolik. He keeps landing fish, you don't, and every one of his catches fills your **rage** bar. At 100 you snap the rod over your knee and jump into the water. Catch the day's quota (in kg) on each of **ten locations** without a meltdown.

- **Locations:** dacha pond, river behind the garages, sand quarry with jet skis, a night on an island (mosquitoes, neighbours with a guitar), the warm power-plant outflow (three-eyed carp), Ashot's paid pond, winter ice fishing, a swamp, a nature reserve with the fishing inspector, and the regional championship final where you also have to out-fish Tolik. Each has its own look, hazards and Tolik's lines.
- **Methods:** *float* — tap when the bobber dips, stop the marker in the green; *feeder* — rod on a pod, keep the feeder topped up by recasting, then a tension fight; *spinning* — hold/release to retrieve the lure in steps, strike, fight; *ice jig* — tap in rhythm to keep the fish interested, strike on the nod. Fights: hold to pull, keep tension in the green, let go before the line snaps.
- **Hazards:** the wife calling, gopniks taking your beer, jet skis and water releases scaring fish, Ashot fining you for your own beer, Tolik buying fish and passing them off, cracking ice (three beers and you go through), leeches, the fishing inspector (no drinking or stealing while he's around), a chanting crowd, a blogger's drone.
- **Progress:** fish sell for money, stars pay a bonus; buy rods (telescopic, feeders, spinning rod, carbon), reels and baits/lures that unlock as you advance. Progress is saved on the device.
- **Coping:** beer (big relief, drunker), cigarettes (slow, also keep mosquitoes away and warm you on the ice), TikTok, and stealing from Tolik's keepnet while he isn't looking.

Rules live in `fishing/src/sim.ts` (pure, no Phaser) and are covered by `tests/fishing.test.mjs`, including a bot balance check. 3D models are original low-poly geometry built in code with flat shading and an ink outline (`fishing/src/three/models.ts`, scene in `fishing/src/Scene3D.ts`); all audio is generated at runtime, so there are no third-party assets to license: sound effects and river/bird/cricket ambience are synthesized with WebAudio (`sfx.ts`), the background music is a generative Karplus–Strong guitar that speeds up and adds a pulse and heartbeat as rage rises (`music.ts`), and voice lines (Tolik, the hero, a CoD-style announcer calling bites and critical rage) use the device's built-in Russian speech synthesis (`voice.ts`; silent on devices without a Russian voice). Sounds, music and voices can each be switched off in the pause menu.

**Better voices (ElevenLabs).** All 133 spoken lines live in `fishing/src/lines.ts`. Record them once (≈3,200 characters) and commit the result:

```bash
ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=sk_... npm run voices        # writes public/fishing/voice/*.mp3 + manifest.json
npm run voices -- --list                          # lines and character count
VOICE_TOLIK=<id> npm run voices -- --force        # re-record with your own voice picks
```

The shipped recordings (`public/fishing/voice/`, 133 lines, ~3.3 MB, trimmed and loudness-normalised) were made with ElevenLabs `eleven_v4` and inline direction tags: Tolik — *Alex, Calm Russian Analyst*; the hero — *Mikhail*; the announcer — *Nester Surovy*. The game plays a recording when one exists and falls back to device speech otherwise. The API key is only used by the script and never ships with the game. For playtesting in the browser console: `__fishing.sim.rage = 95`.

Desktop keys: Space strike/hold to reel · 1 beer · 2 smoke · 3 TikTok/next · X close phone · hold 4 steal · P pause.

## «Розшук» (rozshuk)

A third vertical slice in the same toolchain, in Ukrainian: a roguelite + survival + QTE game rendered in low-poly 3D with Three.js. Served at `/mobile-roguelite/rozshuk/`.

You are wanted by the draft office (ТЦК) and have to last **30 days** in your flat. The month is **15 levels** (two days each): a morning at home, then an outing (вилазка) — take out the trash, buy food, pick up a laptop charger, fetch a power station in a blackout, rescue a friend's cat from a raided market. After day 14 the story **forks**:

- **Кордон (illegal):** cheaper (~70 000 ₴) but harder — a guarded station, a raid on the night train, a village with dogs, a forest watched by a thermal drone, and a freezing river crossing.
- **Офіційно (legal):** a fixer arranges a job at a "critical" company, a deferment and a business trip — ~170 000 ₴ of remote work and visits to the bank, ЦНАП and even the draft office itself; with papers in hand a document check can be passed, and the border booth asks questions only the right papers answer.

- **Home (survival):** money, food stock, satiety, nerves and heat (розшук). Three actions a day: remote work (a ticket-closing timing minigame), eat, sleep, read the «Де бус?» chat to see patrol routes, call mum, push-ups, pet the cat. Random events (blackouts, scam calls, a parcel from mum, rising prices) and, at high heat, a knock on the door — hold your breath.
- **Outing (stealth):** patrols see in cones that turn yellow → red; standing still in a bush or doorway hides you; sprinting is loud; grannies on benches phone it in; vans unload officers; curfew brings police. Get caught and a QTE chain decides: wriggle free (mash), talk your way out (swipe), show your deferment (choice), then jump out of the van (mash). Fail and the run ends — «Бусифікація».
- **Roguelite:** per-run seeds shuffle bushes, objective spots and extra patrols (more with heat); pick one of three perks after every level (sneakers, black hoodie, a dog, parkour, a lawyer on speed dial…); finished runs bank experience that unlocks starting traits (IT guy, athlete, quiet one, mummy's boy, an MP's nephew).

Rules are pure TypeScript with no rendering imports — `rozshuk/src/outing.ts` (stealth simulation on ASCII maps from `maps.ts`), `qte.ts`, `run.ts` (the month, home actions, fork, endings) and `content.ts` (story, levels, perks, events) — covered by `tests/rozshuk.test.mjs`, including checks that every level is reachable and finishable and that a careful player can afford the legal path. All 3D is original low-poly geometry built in code (`rozshuk/src/three/models.ts`) with procedural canvas textures (panel-block facades with lit windows, pixel camo, garages with graffiti, the carpet on the wall), so there are no third-party assets to license; sound effects and the score are synthesized with WebAudio (on iPhones the page declares a media «playback» session so the silent switch does not mute it). All 39 in-game lines (officers' shouts, babusias, the hero, Arkadiy, Molfar, HR, the draft-office clerk and the border guard) are recorded with ElevenLabs `eleven_v4` and Ukrainian library voices, trimmed and loudness-normalised (`public/rozshuk/voice/`, ~0.7 MB); the cast and the prompts with direction tags are in `rozshuk/src/lines.ts`. A line without a recording falls back to the device's Ukrainian speech synthesis. Voices can be switched off in the pause menu; the story chat stays text. Static props are merged per material at load, so a level renders in roughly 100–300 draw calls. Post-processing (`rozshuk/src/three/post.ts`): ground-contact ambient occlusion (GTAO, half resolution on medium), bloom for things that emit light (lit panel-block windows, street lamps with light pools, door bulbs, shop signs and officers' torches at night, van beacons, the drone LED, the laptop and candle at home) and a per-mood colour grade (day, rain, fog, snow, night, indoors, home, blackout) with a red, slightly split edge during a chase. Graphics quality (low / medium / high) is switchable in the pause menu; phones default to medium.

Controls: drag anywhere in the lower part of the screen to walk, hold **БІГ** to sprint, **ПЕРЕЛІЗТИ** appears next to climbable fences. Desktop: WASD/arrows, Shift sprint, E vault, Space/arrows/1–4 in QTEs, P pause. Progress (the run in progress and meta experience) is saved on the device. For playtesting from the console: `__rozshuk.run`, `__rozshuk.sim`.

## «Чотири стіни» (stiny)

A fourth game in the same toolchain, in Ukrainian, served at `/mobile-roguelite/stiny/`. Where «Розшук» is about going out, this one is about never going out: a man without papers who has not left his Kyiv flat since 2022. There is nothing to win. The task is to keep your mind together while every year makes it harder.

- **Structure:** 16 seasons, winter 2023 → autumn 2026. Each season is **one playable day** that stands for the ~90 identical days around it: at night half of what the day gave or took is stretched over the season, minus the weight of the year. The «днів без вулиці» counter grows by ~90 every season.
- **The mind:** *психіка* is the run's health; 0 ends it (*Зрив*). Its **ceiling sinks** every season and drops with every friend who dies, so even the best day is never as good as it used to be. Comforts help less the second time in a day and much less when you are low or apathetic (anhedonia); meds soften both the falls and the joys.
- **The flat (3D, walk with the joystick):** laptop (work: a ticket-closing timing minigame, the only income), phone (the «Де бус?» chat, doomscrolling that costs you at night, friends, mum, food delivery), fridge and stove, the bed (sleep, or lie and stare at the ceiling), the window (the courtyard five floors down: kids and coffee in 2023, empty by 2025, the van and the men in pixel camo later), the balcony (sun — unless the van is there and someone looks up), shower, mirror (the beard grows), a ficus that withers if you forget it, the exercise mat, the shelf, the trash you can never take out (bags pile up in the hall), the calendar, the photos of five friends on the fridge, and the front door. «Вийти на вулицю» is always there; pressing it costs forty minutes of standing with your hand on the lock. When the mind is almost gone it becomes «Вийти. Просто вийти.»
- **Things that happen:** scheduled blackouts and water cuts (no laptop without a power station, no cooking), air-raid alerts with the moped buzz of shahids and explosions near and far (sit in the corridor, between two walls), **ballistic** — eight real seconds to walk to the corridor, delivery couriers you check through the peephole (some are not couriers, some real ones look wrong; open to the wrong one and you have to slam the door), knocks where you hold your breath while they listen, phantom knocks when you are low, mum calling, the neighbour, the drill upstairs, the van in the courtyard.
- **Friends:** each run draws five names and life stories — taken by the metro, volunteered, front, wounded, killed, left abroad, hiding like you. News arrives between seasons; their chats go quiet; a photo gets a black ribbon.
- **What breaking looks like:** colour drains out, a tunnel vignette closes in, the frame pinches with the heartbeat, the walls of the flat creep inwards, the camera narrows and sways, the hero walks slower with his head down, intrusive thoughts surface over the scene, a clock ticks louder, tinnitus, the piano score loses notes and detunes.
- **Roguelite:** a seeded run (friends, schedules, events); after each season pick one of three things that helped (power station, power bank, water jugs, dumbbells, a cat from the shelter, an online therapist, books, a guitar, noise-cancelling headphones, a crate of beer, a European client, a volunteer, the paid «Де бус» channel, tomatoes on the balcony, a diary, antidepressants, the neighbour Petrovych, a console). Seasons lived across runs unlock starting backgrounds (IT guy, tutor, musician, student). Endings: *Зрив*, *Бусифікація*, *Приліт*, and the rare *Дожив*.

Rules are pure TypeScript — `stiny/src/sim.ts` (the run, the day, time, events, resolution, season ends) and `stiny/src/content.ts` (seasons, actions, perks, friends, thoughts, endings) — covered by `tests/stiny.test.mjs`, including a bot balance check (a careful routine survives the four years in roughly a third of runs; pure grinding or doomscrolling never does). The flat, the courtyard and the hero are original low-poly geometry built in code (`stiny/src/Scene3D.ts`), reusing the procedural models and textures of «Розшук» (`rozshuk/src/three/models.ts`); post-processing (`stiny/src/three/post.ts`) adds the `despair` pass on top of the «Розшук» grade. All sound and the score are synthesized at runtime (`stiny/src/audio.ts`). No third-party assets, no voice recordings yet.

Controls: drag in the lower half to walk, tap the action buttons that appear next to things, 📱 for the phone. Desktop: WASD/arrows, E for the first action, P pause. Progress is saved on the device. For playtesting, open the game with `#test` at the end of the address (`/stiny/#test`): a 🧪 button appears during the day with a menu that triggers any event (ballistic, shahed alert, draft-office knock, phantom knock, fake or real courier, mum, neighbour, van, blackout, water cut, drill), changes the state (psyche ±20, evening, food and money) and jumps to the next season or to any year. From the console: `__stiny.run`, `__stiny.scene`.

## «Тікай!» (tikai)

A fifth game in the same toolchain, in Ukrainian, served at `/mobile-roguelite/tikai/`. Where «Розшук» is stealth and «Чотири стіни» is staying in, this one is running: a Temple Run / Subway Surfers–style endless runner turned into a six-stage roguelite route. Behind you — the draft office (ТЦК), the police, the border guards. Ahead — the river.

- **The route:** *Двір* (on foot, panel blocks, babusias on benches), *Ринок* (between the stalls, couriers on scooters), *Об'їзна* (by car — the godfather's beige «копійка», traffic, фури, маршрутки, police on your tail), *Село* (on foot at dusk: dogs, geese, haystacks, carts), *Блокпости* (by tractor, at night: find the open lane between concrete blocks and anti-tank hedgehogs; a barrier arm is not a wall) and *Ліс* (on foot at night: logs, branches, a drone searchlight sweeping the road).
- **On foot:** three lanes, swipe ← → to change lane, ↑ to jump, ↓ to slide. Benches, fences, open manholes and logs are jumped; washing lines, scaffolding and branches are slid under; kiosks, parked cars, stalls and haystacks are only gone around. Puddles, dogs and geese make you stumble. A babusia you run past calls it in unless you are hooded.
- **By car:** four lanes; ↑ is nitro, ↓ honks so the car ahead moves over. Crashes cost a dent (the car has three); scraping a side is only a stumble; close passes pay a bonus; traffic queues up at blockposts.
- **The chase** is one number, the gap to the nearest pursuer: they run a little slower than you, so running clean slowly widens it, while every stumble or crash costs speed and lets them close in. Two stumbles in a row hurt more. Caught = mash «ВИРВАТИСЯ!» if you still have a chance to break free; otherwise the run ends (*Бусифікація*, *Протокол* or *Прикордонники*). Reaching the river is *Річка*.
- **Power-ups:** самокат (faster, absorbs one crash), капюшон (pursuers fall back, babusias stay silent), монобанка (coin magnet), енергетик (smash through everything), and by car — a canister (+1 nitro) and «скотч і молитва» (+1 dent).
- **Roguelite:** seeded tracks; after every stage pick one of three perks (sneakers, a babusia's pie, parkour, a black hoodie, a lawyer on speed dial, the «Де бус?» chat that shows the open lane at blockposts, your own dog, kevlar tyres, a stash in a sock…). Hryvnias picked up on the way are banked after the run and buy who you start as (IT guy, courier, taxi driver, athlete, an MP's nephew); reaching the river unlocks the next level of *розшук* ★ — faster, denser, better paid.

- **The look:** a Subway Surfers–style bent world (a vertex-shader patch on every material bends the road down over the horizon; shadows are computed unbent), cel shading (every material swapped for a 4-tone toon twin), a gradient sky dome with a sun and painted clouds, cartoon characters with knees, elbows and drawn faces animated procedurally (run cycle, jump tuck, slide, stumble, grab, a babusia phoning it in, a soldier waving you to stop), particles (dust from the feet and on landing, sparks on crashes, coin glints, nitro flames, exhaust and smoke from a broken car), hryvnia coins with the ₴ sign, a camera that swoops from the front of the hero to behind him at the start, leans into lane changes and closes in when you are caught, and a blink of invulnerability after a crash.

Rules are pure TypeScript — `tikai/src/sim.ts` (track generation, lanes, jumps and slides, the car, collisions, the chase, power-ups, perks, endings) and `tikai/src/content.ts` (stages, obstacles, perks, traits, lines) — covered by `tests/tikai.test.mjs`, including passability checks (no row on foot is a wall; every blockpost leaves an open lane) and a bot balance check. The world (`tikai/src/Scene3D.ts`, props in `tikai/src/three/props.ts`) is low-poly geometry built in code in the same ink-outline style, reusing the people, vans, panel blocks and textures of «Розшук» and the post-processing of «Чотири стіни»; scenery is nine baked 40 m tiles per stage leap-frogged as you run. Traffic trucks, the motorcycle, roadside forests, coins and finish flags are **Kenney** CC0 models, and the coin, jump, land, fall, break, impact, skid and engine sounds are Kenney CC0 recordings converted to MP3 (`public/tikai/`, see `public/tikai/CREDITS.txt`). Sirens, horns and the score are synthesized; shouts reuse the «Розшук» voice recordings, with the device's Ukrainian speech for lines it does not have.

Controls: swipe anywhere. Desktop: arrows/WASD, Space jump (nitro by car), Shift/S slide (horn by car), P pause; Space/Enter to break free. Progress between stages and the stash are saved on the device. For playtesting, open `/tikai/#test`: a 🧪 button jumps to any stage, gives any power-up, sets the chase gap, ends the stage, adds money or turns on god mode. From the console: `__tikai.run`, `__tikai.stage`.

## «Ставка» (stavka)

A sixth game in the same toolchain, in Ukrainian, served at `/mobile-roguelite/stavka/`. You are the President of Ukraine for one 16-week «каденція». Every week is a day of decisions, a night of air defence in real time and a strike back — on one low-poly 3D map of Ukraine and its neighbours seen from above.

- **Day (Кабінет):** four hours to spend. *Рішення* — one or two cards from ministries, allies and the street, two answers with visible consequences. *Тил* — Лютий drones, Довгий Нептун, decoys, interceptor drones, mobile fire groups, grid repairs, reserves for the front, a ГУР search for hidden enemy air defence. *ППО* — move each battery (Patriot, SAMP/T, IRIS-T, NASAMS, Gepard, mobile groups) to another region once a day; their ranges are drawn on the map. *Союзники* — the US, Germany, Britain, France and Poland, each with trust, a temper, a tolerance for escalation, elections and red lines; ask for missiles, a battery, money, intelligence, long-range weapons or permission to strike Russia, and accept their condition, haggle or decline.
- **Night (Повітряна тривога):** the wave is built from the week and from what you have hit: Shahed swarms with Gerbera decoys, Х-101 from the Caspian, Калібр from the sea, Іскандер and Кинджал on ballistic arcs with a countdown to impact. Mobile groups and Gepard fire by themselves; tap anything in the sky and the cheapest thing that reaches it goes up (an interceptor drone for a Shahed, IRIS-T/NASAMS for a cruise missile, Patriot/SAMP-T for a ballistic one). Two slow-motion charges, ×2 speed. Hits on substations darken whole regions; strikes on batteries can take them out. Every fourth night is a massive strike.
- **Strike (Удар):** eighteen real targets (refineries, arsenals, airbases including Engels, the Alabuga Shahed plant, Novorossiysk, the Crimean bridge, occupied depots), twelve enemy air-defence zones (some hidden until reconnaissance finds them). Pick a target and a weapon, set the count and the decoys, tap up to three waypoints to fly around the zones, read the risk (range, expected arrivals, escalation, which ally you would anger) and watch the swarm fly. A target knocked out weakens the enemy for a few weeks — fewer Shaheds, fewer Х-101 or Калібри, less pressure on one part of the front. Allied weapons on Russia proper without permission, and broken promises, cost trust.
- **The run:** energy, morale, budget, escalation, allies' trust and the front line, which really moves on the map (the occupied land behind it is shaded by a mask that follows it). Lose by two nights of national blackout, by morale at zero or by a breakthrough; survive the 16 weeks for one of three endings depending on the position you negotiate from. After every week one perk out of three; advisors (Генерал ППО, Інженер-дронщик, then Дипломат and Економіст) and Ескалація ★ levels unlock between runs.

Rules are pure TypeScript — `stavka/src/sim.ts` (the week, the wave and interceptions, strike routes and risk, allies and conditions, the front, endings) and `stavka/src/content.ts` (regions, threats, air defence, weapons, targets, zones, allies, decisions, perks, advisors) — covered by `tests/stavka.test.mjs`, including a bot that should usually last 16 weeks and a lazy one that should lose. Numbers are game numbers, not real specifications; strikes only target military and industrial sites. The map (`stavka/src/geo.ts`) is Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain, via world-atlas), clipped and simplified, with Crimea drawn as Ukraine. The world (`stavka/src/Scene3D.ts`) and every model — Shahed, Х-101, Калібр, Іскандер, Кинджал, Лютий, Нептун, Фламінго, Storm Shadow, ATACMS, the air-defence vehicles, Tu-95s, warships, refineries, depots — are low-poly geometry built in code (`stavka/src/three/models.ts`) in the ink-outline toon style of the other games, with the post-processing of «Чотири стіни». There are no voices: text, a synthesized air-raid siren, launches, machine-gun bursts, blasts and the drone of Shaheds overhead.

Controls: drag to move the map, pinch or wheel to zoom, tap to select. For playtesting, open `/stavka/#test`: a ТЕСТ button jumps to any week, adds money or stock, reveals air defence, sets trust, gives every perk, makes missiles endless, starts or ends the night, or loses the run. From the console: `__stavka.run`, `__stavka.night`, and `__stavka.speed = 10` to run the simulation faster.

## «З гірки!» (zhirky)

A seventh game in the same toolchain, in Ukrainian, served at `/mobile-roguelite/zhirky/`. A sledding runner in the spirit of Sled Surfers, turned the other way round: no catapult to upgrade one centimetre at a time, no tickets, no ads. You are a kid with a sled, it is Святвечір, and the shortest way to grandma's is downhill — six slopes of a branching winter route, with as many neighbours' kids as you can pick up hitched behind you.

- **The ватага** is the core: kids waving by the slope hitch their sleds on behind you when you ride past. Each rides your exact track — your jumps, your ducks, your ramps — but swings into a new lane a little later than you do. Swerve late around a snowman and you get past; the kids behind clip it and everyone from that kid back tumbles into the snow. More kids = more candies (+15% each, and a bonus for every kid you bring to the bottom), and a longer tail to protect. A crash of your own costs a heart and half the ватага. Hearts and the ватага carry over from slope to slope.
- **On the slope:** three lanes, swipe ← → to change lane, ↑ to jump, ↓ to lie flat on the sled. Logs, benches, cables and ice holes are jumped; branches, washing lines and booms are ducked under; snowmen, spruces, stalls, fishermen, haystacks and generators are only gone around; drifts, sheep and bare cobbles slow you down. A ramp throws you high — swipe ↑ in the air for a flip, but land it or crash. An ice sheet across the slope speeds you up and will not let you steer. Power-ups: Мішок Миколая (candy magnet), Петарда (smash through everything, the kids safe), Зірка колядників (+2 kids), Термос (+1 heart).
- **The push-off** replaces the catapult: «раз, два — ГОП!» — tap in time with the ватага pushing, and a perfect rhythm starts you faster.
- **The route** is a map of six rows with forks, generated per run: Двір хрущовки, Андріївський узвіз (cobbles, tourists with selfie sticks, stalls), Замерзлий Дніпро (ice, holes, fishermen — a nod to «Не клюёт»), Карпатський ліс, Полонина (sheep, haystacks, колиби, mountains at sunset) and, always last, Блекаут — night in grandma's village, lit only by garlands on generators and your torch — ending at her house. Some nodes add a twist: Хуртовина (blizzard: you see a few rows ahead, candies ×1.5), Відлига (thaw: slush in the lanes, heavy drifts), Колядники (+2 kids and kids everywhere) or a Ярмарок (no slope: the run's candies buy mittens).
- **Roguelite:** after every slope, choose one of three mittens (perks: grandma's вареники, a thermos of узвар, a loud carol that saves your tail once, waxed runners, a sweater with deer for flips, a long rope for more kids, dad's вушанка…). Candies are banked in the Комора after every run, won or lost, and buy heroes (Соломійка, Остап, Бабця Ганя on her own ночви, the dog Бровко who pulls the sled), rides with their own physics (санчата; ночви — fast, spin on ice, steer unwillingly; a tube that bounces off obstacles instead of crashing; a sheet of plywood — fastest, one heart less) and upgrades (a neighbour's kid at the start, an extra heart, a fourth mitten, uncle's map that shows two rows ahead). Getting home unlocks the next level of Мороз ★ — faster, denser, sweeter.

Rules are pure TypeScript — `zhirky/src/sim.ts` (the route map, slope generation, lanes, jumps, ducking, ramps and flips, ice and slush, the ватага following your recorded track, power-ups, mittens, the fair, endings) and `zhirky/src/content.ts` (themes, obstacles, power-ups, mittens, heroes, rides, the Комора, lines) — covered by `tests/zhirky.test.mjs`: the route always runs yard → blackout with no dead ends, no row is a wall, nothing hard sits on ice, kids jump where you jumped, a late swerve gets you past but cuts the tail, and a bot balance check (a decent bot gets home about 40% of the time, a careless one almost never). The world (`zhirky/src/Scene3D.ts`, props in `zhirky/src/three/props.ts`) is low-poly geometry built in code in the same ink-outline toon style, with the bent world, sky and particles of «Тікай!», its cartoon people re-posed to sit on sleds, the buildings and trees of «Розшук» under snow, and the post-processing of «Чотири стіни». Kids' rigs are slimmed (frozen limbs, merged joints, outlines only on head and body) so an eleven-kid ватага stays cheap to draw. Sound effects and the finish flags are the Kenney CC0 files «Тікай!» already ships (see `public/zhirky/CREDITS.txt`); the hiss of the runners, sleigh bells and the push-off beats are synthesized. The score (`zhirky/src/music.ts`) is a generative arrangement of Leontovych's «Щедрик» (public domain) — plucked harp, glassy bells, a string pad, bass, sleigh bells and soft drums on a hall reverb; slow at the menus, a driving 3/4 on the slope that adds layers as the ватага grows. Riders (`zhirky/src/three/sledder.ts`) are chubby winter kids in puffer jackets, knitted beanies and mittens, with painted faces.

Controls: swipe anywhere; tap in time for the push-off. Desktop: arrows/WASD, Space to jump (and flip in the air), Shift/S to duck, P pause. Progress between slopes and the Комора are saved on the device. For playtesting, open `/zhirky/#test`: a 🧪 button jumps to any slope, gives any power-up, fills the ватага, ends the slope, adds candies or turns on god mode. From the console: `__zhirky.run`, `__zhirky.stage`, `__zhirky.advance(seconds)` to run the game forward in fixed steps.


## «Хата з краю» (hata)

An eighth game in the same toolchain, in Ukrainian, served at `/mobile-roguelite/hata/`. Where every other game here is third-person or top-down with ink outlines, this one is first person, physically lit and calm: a cleaning roguelite. A client gives you a neglected house and three working days; every percent you clean pays, the money buys better machines, and the client's stars decide whether your crew moves on to the next house.

- **The plot (~32×45 m, open to walk around):** four facades and two roof slopes (moss), seven windows, five rooms with floors to mop, a bathroom (tiles, tub, basin, toilet), a kitchen with base and wall cabinets, a granite counter, backsplash, sink, stove, fridge and a drying rack, a front lawn and a back yard with uncut grass, fallen leaves under a big tree, a neglected pool (green water, algae, leaves, a rubber duck), a deck, a driveway with oil stains, a path, a back fence with peeling paint, a haystack, trash, coins in the grass, and broken roof tiles.
- **Dirt you can see go:** every cleanable face is a `CleanSurface` (`hata/src/clean/surface.ts`) with its own dirt mask a few centimetres per texel; the tool brushes into it where the crosshair hits and the grime shader (`hata/src/world/grime.ts`) reads it — a heavy film, blotches, run-off streaks, mud splashed up from the ground, moss on faces that look at the sky, a wet sheen that dries. The lamp button paints what's left in pulsing orange. Peeling paint is two-stage: scrape, then paint.
- **Tools (hotbar):** pressure washer (two nozzles), mower (pushed on the lawn; mows real stripes — `hata/src/clean/lawn.ts`, chunked instanced grass with per-blade heights), rake → blower-vac (leaves fly into the bag — `leaffield.ts`), pool skimmer, mop, window squeegee, scraper and roller, metal detector (beeps faster when closer; the client's lost keys/ring are in the haystack), and the rag/hands (wipe, pick up trash, patch the roof, wash the dishes). Washing up is its own mode at the sink: one dish at a time, scrub with your finger, it goes to the rack (`hata/src/dishes.ts`). The ladder takes you onto the roof.
- **Roguelite:** a working day is 10 minutes from 08:00 to 20:00 (the sun moves); after three evenings the client takes the house. Stars at 35/50/65/80/92% overall pay a bonus; zero stars ends the crew's run. After each house pick one of three perks (coffee, holey pockets, the neighbour's kid who rakes for you, good weather, word of mouth, calluses, foreman, a friend at the garage, night shift, a nose for metal). Money and machines are kept between runs; every tool has 2–4 levels in the garage.

Rules are pure TypeScript — `hata/src/content.ts` (tools and levels, pay rates, perks, clients, stars) and `hata/src/sim.ts` (save, buying, the ledger that pays per percent, the day, hand-over, perks) — covered by `tests/hata.test.mjs`. Textures, HDRI sky and furniture/garden models are Poly Haven CC0 assets fetched by a GitHub Action (`scripts/hata-fetch-assets.py`, list in `hata/assets-wanted.json`, recompressed JPEGs in `public/hata/ph/`); the first-person tools and gloves are modelled by a Blender script (`hata/blender/tools.py` → `public/hata/models/tools.glb`); the house, kitchen, trees, grass, leaves, haystack and dishes are built in code. All sound is synthesized (`hata/src/audio.ts`). See `public/hata/CREDITS.txt`.

Controls: left part of the screen — walk (floating stick), the rest — look, the big button — work (it changes to «Підняти», «Лізти», «Мити посуд»… when you aim at something to use, and offers the right tool when you aim at a surface it can't clean). Desktop: WASD, mouse (click to lock), hold left mouse/Space to work, 1–9 tools, H highlight dirt, Tab checklist, B garage, Q nozzle, P pause. Graphics quality (low/medium/high) is in the settings; phones default to medium. Progress is saved on the device. For offline checks: open with `?still=1` and drive it from the console with `__hata.teleport(x, z, yaw, pitch)`, `__hata.setTool('washer')`, `__hata.simulate(seconds, holdAction, [strafe, forward], [lookX, lookY])`, `__hata.rows()`.
